Insurance

Clancy does not take insurance. If you use a Health Savings Account, your HSA card is welcome.

Sliding scale

Depending on current availability and circumstances, a sliding scale may be available for clients.

Good Faith Estimate

You have the right to receive a Good Faith Estimate explaining how much your care will cost. Ask for one before your first session, or learn more at cms.gov/nosurprises.

Attendance

Therapy works best with consistent attendance. Three cancellations or no-shows within 24 hours of a session will result in a referral to another provider.

Fee questions

The fine print

Clancy does not take insurance but accepts all other forms of payment. Many clients pay with a Health Savings Account (HSA) card.

Depending on current availability and circumstances, a sliding scale may be available for clients. Ask about it when you reach out; you can also note it in the Get Started form.

Under the federal No Surprises Act, people who are not using insurance have the right to receive a Good Faith Estimate of the expected cost of care. You can ask for one before scheduling.

Three cancellations or missed appointments within 24 hours of the scheduled session will result in a referral to another provider, based on clinical judgment and practice needs.
