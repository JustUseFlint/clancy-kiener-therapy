A quieter kind of therapy

Together, we can explore where you want to be, and how to get there.

Clancy Kiener is a Licensed Marriage and Family Therapist with fifteen years in practice, licensed in Tennessee and Oregon. Her focus is authentic communication: helping people say what is actually true, and be heard.

Therapy here looks at the whole picture: your relationships, your history and the pressures of everyday life. You will be met where you are, by someone who is direct, warm and practical.

Meet Clancy

Direct, warm, and on your side

Clancy is a Licensed Marriage and Family Therapist with a Doctor of Ministry from George Fox University, a master’s degree, and an undergraduate degree in business, which lets her integrate practical insight with relational and spiritual care.

She works with people from diverse backgrounds on relationships, family patterns, trauma, anxiety, addiction and growth. She is also a professional speaker and the author of Peace and Joy Guidebook.

  • Licensed in Tennessee (1168) and Oregon (T2367)
