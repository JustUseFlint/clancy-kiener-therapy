Understanding addiction therapy

Start your journey toward recovery with one-on-one addiction therapy. Clancy offers compassionate support and personalized care to help you overcome substance use and move toward a healthier, more empowered life. Together, you will create a treatment plan tailored to your unique goals and strengths.

Her experience includes chemical addiction, alcohol use, drug use, chronic relapse and dual diagnosis, where substance use and mental health concerns show up together. Recovery is rarely only about stopping. It is about understanding what the substance has been doing for you, healing what is underneath, and building a life that feels worth protecting.

Clancy also understands how addiction touches families. Where it helps, sessions can explore family of origin, relationships and the trust that needs rebuilding.

Who it is for

  • You are worried about your drinking or drug use
  • You have tried to cut back and found it harder than expected
  • You are in recovery and want ongoing support
  • Relapse has happened and you want to understand it without shame
  • Anxiety, depression or trauma are part of the picture
  • You want an individualized plan, not a one-size program

What sessions look like

The shape of the work

  1. An honest starting point

    Your first session explores your history with substances, what you want, and what has helped or not helped before.

  2. A plan that fits you

    Together you create a treatment plan tailored to your goals and strengths.

  3. Understanding triggers

    Sessions look at the situations, feelings and relationships connected to use, and build alternatives.

  4. Sustaining change

    Work turns toward relapse prevention, repair in relationships, and building a meaningful life in recovery.

Approach & modalities

How Clancy works

Addiction therapy combines Cognitive Behavioral and Behavioral approaches with Solution Focused, Person-Centered and Family Systems perspectives, so recovery is understood in the context of your whole life.

  • CBT
  • Behavioral
  • Solution Focused Brief Therapy
  • Person-Centered
  • Family Systems
