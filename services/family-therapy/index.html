Family Therapy in Nashville

Families are systems. When one part is hurting, everyone feels it. Family therapy helps you understand the pattern you share and change it together.

Understanding family therapy

Family therapy looks at the relationships between people rather than treating any one person as “the problem.” As a Licensed Marriage and Family Therapist trained in family systems, Clancy helps families see how each person’s reactions fit together, and how small changes from several people can shift the whole household.

Clancy has found that family of origin often has a deep impact on mental health. Patterns of communication, roles, unspoken rules and old hurts tend to repeat across generations until someone names them. Family sessions create a place to do that naming safely and respectfully.

Work may include parents and adult children, siblings, blended families, or a parent and partner navigating parenting decisions together. The focus is practical: calmer conversations, clearer expectations and more room for each person to be themselves.

Who it is for

  • Families stuck in recurring conflict
  • Parents who disagree about how to parent
  • Blended families finding a new rhythm
  • Adult children and parents working through old hurts
  • Families affected by addiction, grief or a major transition
  • Households supporting a neurodivergent family member

What sessions look like

The shape of the work

  1. Understanding the family

    The first session gathers each person’s view of what is happening and what they hope will change. Clancy listens for the pattern, not for who is to blame.

  2. Mapping roles and rules

    Together you look at the roles people play, the unspoken rules of the household, and how history from earlier generations shows up today.

  3. Practicing new conversations

    Sessions become a place to try different ways of talking and listening, with support in the room.

  4. Carrying it home

    Each phase ends with small, specific practices the family can try between sessions.
