Understanding anxiety counseling

Anxiety can affect your focus, sleep, and ability to enjoy daily life. In therapy, Clancy helps you identify what is fueling your anxiety and teaches practical tools to manage it.

You will work together to build better coping skills, reduce overthinking, and face challenges with more confidence. Her approach is clear, direct, and focused on real-life progress.

Anxiety rarely appears out of nowhere. It is often connected to stress, old experiences, family patterns or a nervous system that learned to stay on alert. Understanding where it comes from makes it far less frightening, and makes change feel possible.

Who it is for

  • Your mind will not switch off at night
  • Worry or overthinking takes up much of your day
  • Social situations leave you drained or dreading them
  • Stress at work or home is affecting your sleep and focus
  • You avoid things you used to enjoy
  • You want practical tools, not just reassurance

What sessions look like

The shape of the work

  1. Getting specific

    Your first session looks at when anxiety shows up, what it feels like, and what you would like to be different.

  2. Finding the fuel

    Together you identify the thoughts, situations and history that keep anxiety going.

  3. Learning tools

    Using CBT and other approaches, you practice ways to calm the body, question anxious thoughts and reduce avoidance.

  4. Facing challenges

    You take on real situations step by step, building confidence that lasts.

Benefits

What can change

Approach & modalities

How Clancy works

Anxiety counseling leans on Cognitive Behavioral Therapy, which explores how thoughts shape emotions, together with Solution Focused Brief Therapy and Behavioral approaches for steady, practical progress.

  • CBT
  • Solution Focused Brief Therapy
  • Behavioral
  • Person-Centered
