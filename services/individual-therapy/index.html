Understanding individual therapy

Step into a space designed for your growth. In one-on-one sessions, you will find more than just coping strategies. You will uncover what is beneath the surface, explore what truly matters to you, and begin shaping a life with greater clarity, purpose, and self-trust.

Clancy works with individuals from diverse backgrounds, offering support that meets you where you are. Her experience includes stress, depression, social anxiety, trauma, grief, neurodivergence, ADHD, women’s issues and spirituality. She has found that both family of origin and workplace dynamics often have a deep impact on mental health, so both are welcome topics in the room.

With compassionate, personalized support, therapy becomes a place not just to get through, but to grow through. Together, you can explore where you want to be, and how to get there.

Who it is for

  • You feel stuck, overwhelmed or unlike yourself
  • Stress or workplace dynamics are wearing you down
  • You are navigating depression, grief or a major change
  • You want to understand how your family history shapes you
  • You are neurodivergent or living with ADHD and want support that fits
  • You want to reconnect with creativity, faith or purpose

What sessions look like

The shape of the work

  1. Your first session

    You will discuss what is bringing you to therapy and begin identifying goals that matter to you.

  2. Understanding what is beneath

    Sessions explore the thoughts, feelings and history that keep certain patterns in place. Clancy often uses CBT to look at how thoughts shape emotions and day-to-day experience.

  3. Building new patterns

    You will practice ways to challenge unhelpful thinking and respond to life with more choice and steadiness.

  4. Growing through

    As you feel more in control and experience meaningful change, sessions shift toward maintaining growth and planning ahead.

Approach & modalities

How Clancy works
