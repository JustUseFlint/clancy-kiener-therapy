Therapy for couples, families and you

Seven ways to work with Clancy, each with a clear fee. Sessions take place in person at 4235 Hillsboro Pike in Nashville or by telehealth, Monday through Saturday.

Couples & Marriage Therapy

A calm, structured space for two people to slow the conversation down, understand the pattern you keep getting caught in, and find your way back to each other.

$150per couple session

Premarital Counseling

Creating space to strengthen your connection, deepen your understanding, and begin your partnership with clarity and intention.

$4002-hour session

Family Therapy

Families are systems. When one part is hurting, everyone feels it. Family therapy helps you understand the pattern you share and change it together.

$150per session

Individual Therapy

Step into a space designed for your growth. Therapy becomes a place not just to get through, but to grow through.

$15055-minute session

Anxiety Counseling

Let’s quiet the noise and find your center. Clear, direct support focused on real-life progress.

$15055-minute session

Addiction Therapy

Start your journey toward recovery with one-on-one addiction therapy, and move toward a healthier, more empowered life.

Clancy specializes in helping individuals heal from trauma, both environmental and narcissistic, and in supporting people recovering from family abuse.

Not sure where to start?

Choosing the right fit

If it is mostly about your relationship

Start with couples & marriage therapy. If you are engaged or planning to marry, premarital counseling is designed for you.

If the whole household feels it

Family therapy looks at the patterns between people, not one “problem person.”

If it is about you

Individual therapy covers a wide range, with focused work for anxiety, addiction and trauma and family of origin.

Still unsure? The Get Started form takes about two minutes, and Clancy can help you decide.
