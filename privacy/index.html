This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and it does not collect or store anything you type. The Get Started form runs entirely in your browser. When you finish it, you choose whether to send your note by opening your own email app, phone or messaging app, or the online scheduler. Anything you send through those services is handled by those services.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as part of delivering the font files. Links to Amazon, Psychology Today, Facebook and the online scheduler take you to third-party sites with their own privacy policies.

HIPAA notice of privacy practices

This notice describes how medical and mental health information about you may be used and disclosed, and how you can access this information. Please review it carefully.

Your rights

  • Access your records: you can ask to see or receive a copy of your counseling record.
  • Request corrections: you can ask to correct information you believe is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Confidential communications: you can request to be contacted in a specific way (for example, only by phone, not email).
  • Receive a copy of this notice: you can ask for a paper copy at any time, even if you agreed to receive it electronically.
  • File a complaint: if you believe your privacy rights have been violated, you may file a complaint with the practice or with the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services (HHS). Filing a complaint will not affect your services.

Right to confidentiality

At Quiet Mind Therapy, your privacy is of the utmost importance. What you share in counseling is kept confidential in accordance with professional ethics and federal and state laws. Information disclosed in counseling sessions is confidential and will not be shared without your written consent.

Exceptions to confidentiality

  • Imminent danger to self: if you are at risk of harming yourself.
  • Imminent danger to others: if you threaten serious harm to another person.
  • Abuse or neglect: if there is suspicion or disclosure of abuse or neglect of a child, elderly person or vulnerable adult.
  • Court order: if required by law or court order.
  • Your written request to release information to a designated individual or organization.

Our responsibilities

The practice is required by law to maintain the privacy and security of your protected health information (PHI), to follow the duties and privacy practices described in this notice, and to give you a copy upon request. Your information will not be used or shared other than as described here unless you give written authorization, which you may revoke in writing at any time.

Questions or complaints

Email clancy@quietmindtherapy.com or call 615-423-4140. You may also file a complaint with the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, Office for Civil Rights, at 1-877-696-6775 or hhs.gov/ocr/privacy/hipaa/complaints.

Office accessibility

Quiet Mind Therapy is committed to maintaining an environment that is accessible and accommodating to individuals with mobility needs, in the spirit of the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA).

  • Wheelchair-accessible entryways and restrooms
  • Elevator access for offices not located on the ground floor
  • Accessible and comfortable seating in the waiting area

If you encounter any accessibility barrier, in person or online, please let the practice know at clancy@quietmindtherapy.com or 615-423-4140. Every reasonable effort will be made to provide accommodations.

Practice policies

ADA and FMLA paperwork

Clancy does not complete ADA or FMLA paperwork, based on HIPAA privacy requirements and the absence of a secure fax line. A primary care physician or medical provider can handle these forms securely.

Emotional Support Animal letters

Clancy does not provide ESA evaluations or letters.

Court-related services

Clancy does not attend court or provide court testimony, including custody hearings, disability hearings or other legal proceedings, except when she receives a court order. A forensic specialist is the right resource for court-related evaluations.

Attendance

Therapy is most effective when appointments are attended consistently. Three cancellations or missed appointments within 24 hours of the scheduled session will result in a referral to another provider, based on clinical judgment and practice needs. If you need referrals for any of the services above, Clancy is glad to point you toward appropriate professionals.
