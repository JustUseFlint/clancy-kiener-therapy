Back-to-basics guidance for a more joyful life

The Peace & Joy Guidebook offers back-to-basics guidance to help you connect more deeply with yourself and others for a more joyful life. Author Clancy Kiener offers stories and insights she’s gained as a therapist and professional speaker helping people from all walks of life forge their unique paths to attain more joy and peace. She explores how we acquire harmful beliefs and feelings and presents practical strategies for “cleaning house” and connecting with others more authentically. She helps you initiate essential conversations, even if those conversations are with yourself, to resolve beliefs and experiences that are holding you back. You will emerge with a deeper understanding of your inner power and a set of strategies for embracing life and living more joyfully.

About the author

Clancy Kiener

Clancy Kiener is a licensed marriage and family therapist with a master’s degree in religion. She enjoys working as a therapist and professional speaker at treatment centers and churches, connecting with people from all walks of life. In her therapy practice, Clancy focuses on empowerment, dreams, connection, and nutrition.
