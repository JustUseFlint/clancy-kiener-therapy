About Clancy

Clancy Kiener, LMFT, D.Min.

Licensed Marriage and Family Therapist in Nashville, Tennessee. Fifteen years in practice. Licensed in Tennessee and Oregon. Author, professional speaker and a steady, honest presence for the people she works with.

Work with ClancyRead her book

In her words

“Please feel free to reach out. If I am not the right fit we can work together to find a fit that will better suit your needs.”

Meeting you where you are

Clancy specializes in helping individuals heal from trauma, both environmental and narcissistic. Through her work, she has found that both family of origin and workplace dynamics often have a deep impact on mental health. With over fifteen years of experience, she works with individuals from diverse backgrounds, offering support that meets you where you are.

Her practice, Quiet Mind Therapy, is dedicated to guiding people toward improved mental health. Alongside individual and addiction therapy, she offers couples and premarital counseling, complemented by creative processing and professional growth work. Her focus lies in fostering authentic communication and using innovative methods to enhance mental healthcare.

She has years of experience working with people facing social anxiety, trauma, depression, autism, chemical addiction and family issues. She supports those recovering from family abuse and guides clients in reclaiming their creativity as part of the healing process.

Her approach in the room

In a first session, you will discuss what is bringing you to therapy and begin identifying goals that matter to you. Clancy often uses Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), which helps explore how thoughts shape emotions and day-to-day experiences. Together, you work on ways to challenge unhelpful thought patterns so you can feel more in control and experience meaningful change.

As a marriage and family therapist, she also looks at relationships as systems. The way a family communicated, handled conflict or kept secrets often echoes in adult relationships. Seeing those echoes clearly tends to replace blame with understanding, and understanding with choice.

Sessions are available both in person at 4235 Hillsboro Pike in Nashville and via telehealth, and appointments are scheduled Monday through Saturday, most hours.

A personal note

Beyond the office

Clancy lives in Middle Tennessee with her husband and their temperamental but lovable dog, Joey. She is a proud mom to her daughter and enjoys visiting with her mom, who also lives in Tennessee.
