Getting started

Use the short Get Started form on this site, call or text 615-423-4140, email clancy@quietmindtherapy.com, or book directly through the online scheduler. When you book, a reminder is sent by text, email, or both.

In the first session you will discuss what is bringing you to therapy and begin identifying goals that matter to you.

Clancy works with individuals from diverse backgrounds, couples, and families. Her Psychology Today profile lists adults and elders (65+) as her client focus.

That is completely fine. Describe what is going on and Clancy can help you decide. If she is not the right fit, she will work with you to find a better one.

Sessions

Individual, anxiety and addiction sessions are listed at 55 minutes. Premarital counseling is a 2-hour session.

Both. In-person sessions take place at 4235 Hillsboro Pike, Suite 100, Nashville. Telehealth is also available. Clancy is licensed in Tennessee and Oregon.

Monday through Saturday, most hours.

Clancy often uses Cognitive Behavioral Therapy and draws on Family Systems, Family / Marital, Person-Centered, Narrative, Gestalt, Humanistic, Existential, Interpersonal, Object Relations, Behavioral and Solution Focused Brief approaches.

Fees & payment

Individual sessions are $150. Couple sessions are $150. Premarital counseling is $400 for a 2-hour session.

Depending on current availability and circumstances, a sliding scale may be available. Ask when you reach out.

No. Clancy does not take insurance but takes all other forms of payment.

ACH bank transfer, American Express, Apple Cash, cash, check, Discover, Health Savings Account, Mastercard, PayPal, Venmo, Visa, wire and Zelle.

Policies

Therapy is most effective when appointments are attended consistently. Three cancellations or missed appointments within 24 hours of the scheduled session will result in a referral to another provider, based on clinical judgment and practice needs.

No. Because of HIPAA privacy requirements and the lack of a secure fax system, Clancy does not complete ADA or FMLA paperwork. A primary care physician or medical provider can handle these forms securely.

No. Clancy does not provide ESA evaluations or letters.

Clancy does not attend court or provide testimony, including custody or disability hearings, except when she receives a court order. A forensic specialist is the right resource for court-related evaluations.

Privacy & safety

Yes. Information disclosed in counseling is confidential and will not be shared without your written consent, except in the limited situations required by law, such as imminent danger to self or others, suspected abuse or neglect, or a court order.

The Get Started form on this site does not send or store anything. It helps you prepare a note, then hands it to your own email app, phone or the online scheduler.
